# Every year, 3,500 kidneys are discarded while 5,000 patients die waiting.

**Match** is an ML-powered kidney allocation system that treats organ quality as a spectrum — not a binary accept/reject decision. By matching marginal organs to suitable recipients, we can save hundreds of additional lives per year.

## How It Works

| Step | Traditional System | Match System |
|------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Scoring** | Binary KDPI cutoff (accept/reject) | Continuous ML quality spectrum (0-100) |
| **Matching** | Sequential offers (2-4 hours per organ) | Parallel matching (30-60 min) |
| **Marginal organs** | 22% discard rate due to center risk aversion | 8% discard rate — suitable recipients found |
