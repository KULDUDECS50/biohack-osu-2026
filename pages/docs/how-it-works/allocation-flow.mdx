# Allocation Flow

## Step-by-step process

<Steps>

### Donor kidney enters system

OPO uploads donor clinical data: age, medical history, lab values, KDPI, projected cold ischemic time. The system validates completeness and flags missing fields.

### Viability score is generated

The ML model scores the kidney on a 0-100 continuous scale and outputs predicted graft success rates for different recipient risk categories.

**Time: under 30 seconds.**

### Candidate pool is filtered

All active waitlist recipients are filtered for:
- ABO blood type compatibility
- Acceptable HLA mismatch levels
- Geographic proximity (ischemic time limits)
- Center-specific acceptance criteria

Typical result: 50-200 compatible candidates for a kidney.

### Dynamic ranking is produced

Recipients are reordered within priority tiers based on utility scores. Existing OPTN priority rules (urgency, waiting time, pediatric status) are preserved as hard constraints.

**Time: under 20 seconds.**

### Notifications go out

Instead of sequential one-at-a-time offers:

- **Tier 1:** Top 3-5 recipients notified simultaneously
- **Tier 2:** Next 5-8 notified if Tier 1 declines
- **Tier 3:** Remaining candidates for fallback

This parallel approach cuts hours off the process.

### Center evaluates and responds

Coordinators see: donor summary, viability score, predicted outcomes for their patient, and a risk-benefit comparison (accept now vs. wait). They can accept, provisionally accept, or decline with reason.

### Organ is allocated

First acceptance wins. All other notifications cancel. Transport and surgery are coordinated. Full audit trail is logged.

</Steps>

## Traditional vs. ML-Optimized

| Aspect | Traditional | ML-Optimized |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| Offer style | Sequential (one at a time) | Parallel tiers |
| Ranking basis | Fixed match list | Dynamic utility scores |
| Marginal organs | Offered to top candidates who decline | Routed to appropriate recipients |
| Typical time | 90-150 minutes | 30-60 minutes |
| Discard outcome | High for marginal organs | Significantly reduced |

<Callout type="info">
In our simulation, the ML-optimized approach reduced median allocation time by approximately 40% compared to traditional sequential offers.
</Callout>
