# Ethics & Safety

<Callout type="warning">
This system is decision support, not automation. No organ is allocated without a human clinician approving the decision.
</Callout>

## What the system does NOT do

- Does not remove or override any existing OPTN priority rules
- Does not automatically allocate organs without human approval
- Does not use race, income, insurance, or disability as model features
- Does not penalize clinicians for overriding recommendations
- Does not lower acceptance standards — it matches more intelligently

## Fairness protections

### Priority rules are preserved

Medical urgency, pediatric priority, waiting time, and geographic tiers remain as hard constraints. The system reorders recipients **only within** priority tiers, never across them.

### No demographic discrimination

Protected attributes (race, ethnicity, socioeconomic status) are **excluded** from the model. Allocation outcomes are monitored by demographic group to detect indirect bias from correlated features.

If disparities emerge, the algorithm includes corrective weighting mechanisms.

### Position change limits

No recipient can drop more than 10 positions due to utility-based reordering. This prevents extreme deprioritization of any candidate.

## Transparency

Every allocation decision is fully auditable:

- Original match list and priority scores
- ML-generated viability scores and feature importance
- Reordering rationale for each recipient
- Final allocation outcome and clinician response

Clinicians can always see **why** the system made a recommendation, not just what it recommended.

## Human oversight

- Transplant coordinators review all organ offers with full clinical context
- Surgeons can override algorithmic recommendations with documented justification
- Overrides are logged for outcome analysis but never penalized
- If the system is unavailable, allocation reverts to standard protocols

## Limitations we acknowledge

- ML models trained on historical data may encode past biases
- Synthetic data cannot fully replicate real-world complexity
- Model predictions have uncertainty — confidence intervals are always shown
- Algorithmic fairness is an ongoing process, not a solved problem

## Medical Disclaimer

This is a research simulation tool using synthetic data. It is **not** intended for clinical decision-making. Real kidney allocation is governed by OPTN/UNOS and involves complex medical, ethical, and regulatory considerations beyond the scope of this prototype.
