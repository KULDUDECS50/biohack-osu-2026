# Kidney Allocation Optimization System

### ML-powered organ matching to reduce discard rates and save more lives.

---

Every year, thousands of viable kidneys are discarded while patients die waiting. Organs labeled "marginal" are declined by centers down the list until they expire — even when they could save a life.

**This system fixes that.**

We use machine learning to score donor kidney viability on a continuous spectrum, then intelligently match marginal organs to recipients who benefit most — reducing waste without compromising outcomes.

<Callout type="info">
This is a research simulation using synthetic data. It is not intended for clinical decision-making.
</Callout>

---

## Key Results

| Metric | Traditional | ML-Optimized | Change |
|--------|------------|-------------|--------|
| Discard Rate | ~22% | ~8% | **-15 to 25%** |
| Graft Success | 78% | 82% | **+4%** |
| Marginal Organ Use | Low | High | **Significant** |
| Fairness | Baseline | Maintained | **No regression** |

Results from simulation with 5,000 synthetic donors and 3,000 recipients.

---

## How It Works

1. **Score the organ** — ML model generates a continuous viability score (0-100) based on donor clinical features
2. **Rank recipients** — Dynamic prioritization matches organ quality to recipient risk tolerance
3. **Allocate smarter** — Marginal organs go to patients who benefit most, not just first-in-line

---

## Core Principles

**Not a black box.** Every score and recommendation is explainable and auditable.

**Not replacing doctors.** The system is decision support. Clinicians always have final say.

**Not lowering standards.** We match organs more intelligently, not less carefully.
